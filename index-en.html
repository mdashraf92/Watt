For drivers

Four steps, no surprises

Charging happens in the app: find, book, charge, pay.

  1. 01

    Open the map

    See the chargers near you, whether they are free, and each one's price per kWh before you move.

  2. 02

    Book your slot

    Pick a time and a duration. The connector is held for you, so you never drive to a busy charger.

  3. 03

    Scan to start

    Scan the QR code at the charger and it switches on. Watch energy and cost climb live.

  4. 04

    Paid from your wallet

    Top up through Thawani; the session is deducted when it ends. No cash, no arranging with anyone.

Inside the app

What the app actually does

  • Live map and status

    Chargers nearby, which are free, which are under maintenance.

  • Book ahead

    Reserve a time slot instead of queueing.

  • Wallet and Thawani

    Top up by card, or let a saved card cover the gap when your balance runs short.

  • Automatic cut-off

    The session stops itself before it can outrun the amount held on your wallet.

  • Trip planning

    Long drive? The app tells you where you must stop — or says plainly that it is not feasible.

  • Arabic and English

    The app is fully Arabic with real RTL, not a translation layer over an English UI.

The more you charge

  1. Standard 0–9 sessions
  2. Silver 10–49 sessions
  3. Gold 50+ sessions

Tiers give booking priority, faster support and offers. They are counted automatically from your sessions.

How you become a host

  1. 01

    Send your application

    Where the charger is, its power, and a photo of the installation.

  2. 02

    Site visit

    We check the wiring, the safety and whether Wi-Fi reaches the charger.

  3. 03

    Sign and install

    We sign the agreement and fit the smart control unit to your charger.

  4. 04

    Take bookings

    You appear in the app, and each session's earnings land in your balance automatically.

Apply to host a charger

Earnings are added to your balance automatically after each session. Transferring to your bank account happens when you request it and we do it by hand — no automated payout provider exists in Oman yet, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.
