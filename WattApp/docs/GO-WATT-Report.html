1 Executive Summary Enhancement Sprint Delivered

This week we completed the planned enhancement sprint and cleared a major engineering milestone: our two parallel development tracks the UI/UX redesign and the technical/back-end platform were successfully merged into a single, unified production codebase. On top of the previously feature-complete build, we shipped a full investor payout system, host earnings, and a significant security and performance hardening pass. The application now carries the finalized GO WATT brand identity. One recurring cost was also eliminated. The platform is on track to enter formal testing next week.
